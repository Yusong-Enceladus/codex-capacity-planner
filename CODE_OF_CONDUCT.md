# Code of conduct

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Harassment, discrimination,
threats, doxxing, and publication of another person's credentials or private
account data are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and community content
that violate these expectations, and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when necessary to protect contributors and users.

Report conduct or privacy concerns privately to the maintainers rather than
posting sensitive evidence in a public issue.
